Rules of the Arcane Recall

Beyond the gray mist, every card conceals a counterpart. Your task is to unveil the matching pairs before your concentration fades.

  1. Choose two cards each turn to reveal their symbols.
  2. If the symbols match, the pair is claimed and remains revealed.
  3. If they differ, commit their locations to memory; they will be veiled again.
  4. Continue until every pair has been claimed.

Warning: Careless guesses invite confusion. Observe closely, remember everything, and trust neither the first impression nor the darkness between cards.
